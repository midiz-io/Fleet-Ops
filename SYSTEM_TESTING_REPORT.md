## 3. Key Operational Workflows & Testing Outcomes

### A. Driver Registration & Account Setup
- **What Was Done:** All active drivers and fleet managers were registered in the system with secure access credentials.
- **Testing Result:** Drivers can log in quickly using their registered phone numbers and secure PIN codes on mobile devices.

### B. Daily Vehicle Health Checklists
- **What Was Done:** Tested digital daily check-in routines before vehicles leave the premises (checking odometer readings, oil levels, tires, brakes, lights, and meter photos).
- **Testing Result:** Verified that drivers can complete checks in under 2 minutes. Management receives real-time visibility on vehicle readiness and safety compliance.

### C. Fuel Management & Mileage Monitoring
- **What Was Done:** Tested fuel expense recording, fuel receipt photo uploads, and automatic mileage performance (KMPL) calculations.
- **Testing Result:** The system accurately compares actual fuel consumption against target benchmarks, immediately flagging low-performing vehicles or unusual fuel usage.

### D. Digital Repair Request & Maintenance Workflow
- **What Was Done:** Tested driver-initiated repair requests (with photo attachments) and management approval flows.
- **Testing Result:** Managers can review repair needs, approve maintenance work, track ongoing repairs, and maintain automated weekly maintenance schedules.

### E. Executive Cost & Operational Dashboard
- **What Was Done:** Simulated expense tracking across fuel, servicing, and spare parts.
- **Testing Result:** Provides clear visual summaries of overall fleet running costs, helping identify high-cost vehicles and streamline budget allocations.

## 4. Key Benefits for Business Leadership

1. **Complete Fleet Visibility:** Real-time awareness of vehicle availability, maintenance status, and assigned drivers from any location.
2. **Preventive Cost Control:** Early detection of fuel inefficiencies and upcoming service requirements prevents major breakdown costs.
3. **Automated Compliance Reminders:** Automatic monitoring of vehicle license renewals, insurance expiries, and driver license validity.
4. **Paperless Accountability:** Digital records with photo proof (odometers, fuel receipts, damages) ensure complete operational transparency.
